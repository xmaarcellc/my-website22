About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Developer and recent Economics graduate from Montclair State University with a minor in Computer Science. I specialize in building modern web applications using JavaScript, React, PHP, and other cutting-edge technologies. As the founder of Marcell Software Services LLC, I provide comprehensive software development, consulting, and visual content creation services.

Beyond coding, I'm passionate about visual storytelling through photography and videography, with expertise in Adobe Premiere and Photoshop. My professional journey includes leadership roles at Starbucks, where I honed my skills in team management, operational efficiency, and customer experience—even mastering the art of latte design. I channel my creative energy into automotive passion projects, capturing compelling content that brings ideas to life. Whether I'm developing a sleek portfolio website like Elicona Visuals, mentoring aspiring developers, or exploring new technologies, I'm driven by the process of transforming concepts into meaningful realities.
